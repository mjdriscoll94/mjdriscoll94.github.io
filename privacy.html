Privacy Policy

Effective October 29, 2026

Sermon Library is designed to keep your ministry archive on your Mac. It does not sell personal information or upload your indexed archive to a Sermon Library server.

Information the app accesses

Sermon Library accesses files, folders, spreadsheets, and Google Sheets that you explicitly choose to connect. This material may include document text, spreadsheet cells, file names, file locations, dates, teaching topics, scripture references, and names contained in your sources.

How information is used

The app uses connected material to build a searchable local index and answer questions about your preaching and teaching archive. Information is used only to provide the features you request.

Local storage

Indexed document content, spreadsheet data, settings, and search records are stored locally in the app’s data folder on your Mac. Your original files remain in their existing locations.

Google Sheets access

If you connect Google Sheets, Sermon Library requests read-only access to retrieve spreadsheet content and formatting needed for indexing. The app does not edit, create, or delete your Google Sheets. Google authorization credentials are stored using protections provided by macOS.

Sharing and sale of information

Sermon Library does not sell your personal information. The app does not send your indexed archive to a Sermon Library-operated cloud service. Google data is not used for advertising.

Your choices

You can remove connected folders, uploaded spreadsheets, and Google Sheets from the app. You can also revoke Google access through your Google Account security settings. Removing a source stops future syncing and removes that source from the local research library.

Data retention

Locally indexed information remains on your Mac until you remove the connected source, clear the app’s data, or uninstall the app and delete its local data. Google access credentials remain until you disconnect the account or revoke access.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as Sermon Library changes. The effective date above identifies the current version.

Contact and support

For privacy questions or support, contact the person or organization that provided your Sermon Library download.
