How it works

Research the work you have already done.

Sermon Library is built for pastors, teachers, and ministry teams who need reliable answers from their own material.

01

Connect your sources

Choose local folders, upload compatible spreadsheets, or connect selected Google Sheets.

02

Build a private index

The desktop app organizes sermons, lessons, dates, passages, teachers, and source locations on your Mac.

03

Ask in plain language

Search notes, teaching schedules, or both—and open the source behind every answer.

Private by design

Your archive stays yours.

Your document text, spreadsheet rows, and search index are stored locally by the desktop app. Sermon Library does not upload your archive to a Sermon Library server.

When you connect Google Sheets, the app requests read-only access and only reads spreadsheets you choose to connect. You can disconnect at any time.
